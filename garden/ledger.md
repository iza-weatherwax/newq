# Ledger

What the story has invented so far. Claude keeps this up to date after every
scene; the author never has to touch it (but can).

Marks: `~` provisional (made up on the fly, can be bent or dropped) ·
`✓` settled (the author confirmed it or built on it themselves).

## Premise (from the author)

- ✓ Ymir, a paladin sworn to the gods, goes alone to slay a beast for the northern duke, to win his support for when the newest queen takes the throne.
- ✓ On the way she meets Sage, a priestess on a pilgrimage, who follows her out of curiosity. They travel together for 4 weeks.
- ✓ Ymir really wants to stay away from the capital: a princess is pushing the king to let her marry Ymir. Ymir hopes serving in the duke's guard for a while will make the king distrust her enough to call it off. She confesses this slowly, over days.
- ✓ Sage's pilgrimage is part of an ancient process for choosing the next high priestess. She doesn't want the role, so she's taking her time and doing it all wrong. When Ymir asks why she's there, "pilgrimage" is the true answer.
- ✓ The beast is an animal corrupted by black vines growing inside a cave. Sage burns as much as she can; Ymir blocks the entrance.
- ✓ In the nearest village: a hot meal, a hot bath, a shared bed. Sage leaves first and leaves a small wrist brace as a gift.
- ✓ Ymir returns to the capital with the duke's thanks. The king refuses to let her join the duke's guard. Ymir thinks it's over.

## How the world works

- ~ Paladins are the "Sworn of the Dawn". They don't lie: their sword warms when they get close to a lie, "like it's embarrassed". (001)
- ~ The Dawn's virtues are numbered: honesty is first, sharing is fourth. (001)
- ~ A "Council" picks missions for paladins. (001)
- ~ In the capital, sending someone lemons in winter declares romantic intentions. (001)
- ~ Reading after bells counts as vanity in the novitiate. (001)
- ~ The north has hunters, and the duke has soldiers ("mean"). (001)
- ~ Forest creatures not in any book: a stag with glass antlers that chime, a bird that sings songs backwards, lights in the river that swim upstream, a thing in the reeds with too many elbows (it returned the worm, folded), a small grey animal that holds a pinecone like a hat and nods. (001)
